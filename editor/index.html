EpiPalace Map Editor

How to use the map editor

  1. Pick a tool on the left (or press its key), then click or drag on the grid. North is up.
  2. Select (V): click anything to select it. Drag to move it; drag the white squares to resize. Moving a room moves everything inside it. Drag empty space to pan; scroll to zoom.
  3. Change numbers (heights, sizes, names) in the panel on the right.
  4. Doors: rooms must touch. Pick the Door tool and click on the wall the two rooms share.
  5. Stairs and ramps: drag from the bottom to the top. Set the top height in the panel.
  6. ▶ Preview walks through your level in a new tab. Select a room first to start inside it.
  7. Your work is saved automatically in this browser. To keep a copy, use Download palace.js.

Put your level on epipalace.com

  1. Click Download palace.js. The file goes to your Downloads folder.
  2. On GitHub, open the repository epipalace, then the folders src → level.
  3. Click Add file → Upload files, and drag in the downloaded palace.js. It must keep exactly that name.
  4. Below the upload box, choose Create a new branch, then click Propose changes, then Create pull request.
  5. Wait for the checks to turn green. Netlify runs the collision tests on your level. Then click Merge pull request.

Or just send the downloaded file to Claude and ask for it to be published.
